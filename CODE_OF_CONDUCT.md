# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

GitHub Repo Doctor welcomes a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
community. Contributors and maintainers are expected to help make participation safe
and productive for people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, respectful feedback and critique ideas rather than people.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and repair their impact.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, spam, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and
when someone publicly represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may issue a warning, remove content, temporarily restrict participation,
or permanently exclude someone according to the severity and pattern of behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately using a contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/3xpp).
Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. If a concern involves the maintainer or
GitHub-hosted abuse, use GitHub's built-in abuse-reporting tools.
